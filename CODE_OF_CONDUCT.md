# Code of conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want this space to be welcoming and useful for everyone who uses Noduct, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Stay on topic: problems, ideas and questions about Noduct.
- Give feedback on the product, not on people.
- Respect privacy: yours and other people's.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or sexualized language or images.
- Spam, advertising, or posts made only to promote something.
- Posting someone else's private information, or documents that contain it.
- Links or attachments meant to trick people into downloading or running something.
- Repeatedly opening the same request after it was closed.

## Enforcement

The maintainers of Noduct moderate this repository. Depending on the case, we may:
- edit or hide a post;
- close or lock an issue;
- limit interactions in the repository;
- block an account.

Automatic triage only adds labels or closes issues that skip the required confirmation. People make every other moderation decision.

To report behavior that breaks these rules, use **Report content** on the post. If you'd rather not do that publicly, open a private report from the **Security** tab and mention that it is about conduct.

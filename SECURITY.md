# Security policy

Noduct processes files locally in your browser, but a flaw in the site could still put people at risk. If you find one, please report it **privately**.

## How to report

1. Open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. This creates a private report that only the maintainers of Noduct can see.
2. Describe what you found, the affected page or tool, the version shown in the site footer, and the steps to reproduce.
3. Please don't open a public issue, discussion or comment about it until a fix is released.

Examples of what we want to hear about:
- a file leaving the browser;
- a script injected through a page or a file;
- data from the Local Files area being readable by another site;
- a way around a privacy promise made on the site.

## What to expect

- Your report is reviewed as soon as possible.
- When a fix is possible, it ships as a new version, listed in [Releases](../../releases).
